# Group 29 Collaboration Workspace - Aprendizaje No Supervisado (MIAD)

## About Group 29
This folder serves as the central collaboration zone for Group 29 in the "Aprendizaje No Supervisado" (Unsupervised Learning) course, part of the Maestría en Inteligencia Analítica de Datos (MIAD) at Universidad de los Andes. As part of our mandatory group work, this workspace is dedicated to building, refining, and packaging our shared workshops, code deliverables, and video presentations.

## Course & Project Context
The curriculum focuses on models designed for data that lacks an observed response or target variable[cite: 1]. Throughout the course, our team is responsible for collaborative deliverables across multiple modules, including:
* **Dimensionality Reduction:** Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)[cite: 1].
* **Clustering Algorithms:** K-medias, K-medoides, Hierarchical Clustering, and DBSCAN[cite: 1].
* **Recommendation Systems:** Collaborative filtering, market basket analysis, and Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)[cite: 1].
* **Geographical Analysis:** Bivariate kernel density estimation, spatial hot spots, and Gaussian mixture models[cite: 1].

## Group Workflow & Assessment Structure
Per the course syllabus, our collaborative assignments are split into two evaluation types[cite: 1]:
1. **Tutor-Evaluated Workshops (Modules 1, 3, 5, 7):** These carry a significant weight (16% each: 12% video deliverable, 4% Jupyter notebook) and are graded by the course tutors based on both product and team performance[cite: 1].
2. **Peer-Evaluated Workshops (Modules 2, 4, 6):** These account for 8% each and require group submissions that will be reviewed and graded by other student teams using course rubrics[cite: 1].
